A private correspondence

K & I

My dearest Kristina,

Will you do me the honor of being my Valentine?

In every chapter of my life, your name is still my favorite line.

If this heart were a novel, its one inevitable ending would be us.

Volume I

Observations of a Most Fortunate Husband

Once, there was a librarian with a soft heart and steel-level grace, and a man who realized very quickly he was done for.

Love did not arrive with noise. It arrived in long talks, shared meals, and the gentle certainty that home can be a person.

Then came the night beneath a meteor shower, when one question changed every future chapter. You said yes. The stars are still recovering.
